# Eric Nyeste

**Normative and AI Ethics | Human–AI Interaction and Evaluation Research | Pedagogy and Coaching**

*Agency, Discourse, Rupture.*

I'm a graduate researcher in Philosophy at San Francisco State University, investigating the relationships among human agency, conversational control, and the evaluation of AI-mediated discourse.

My research explores how philosophical questions about epistemic agency, technological influence, and human judgment can be translated into observable phenomena and empirical evaluation methods.

My interdisciplinary approach draws on continental philosophy, critical theory, discourse analysis, and machine learning to investigate how humans interact with increasingly capable AI systems.

## Current Research

### AI Behavioral Relativity
**Human–LLM Interaction | Conversational Dominance | Model Evaluation**

Leading an interdisciplinary research project developing a framework for evaluating conversational dominance and shifts in user agency during sustained human–LLM interactions.

The experimental design incorporates human-annotated reference labels, linguistic feature identification, comparative model evaluation, and quantitative classification metrics.

*Status: Experimental design and methodology development.*

### Machine Learning Classification and Evaluation
**Python | scikit-learn | Random Forest | Experimental Design**

Developed an exploratory supervised-learning pipeline examining the classification of single-cell gene-expression data.

The project investigates model performance, cross-validation, feature selection, dimensionality reduction, and the methodological tradeoffs between classification accuracy and model complexity.

The accompanying research report also documents the use of generative AI in scientific learning, programming, and experimental interpretation.

[Explore the ML Pipeline Project](https://github.com/eric-nyeste/ML-Pipeline-Project-Random-Forest-Scikit.learn)

### AETHOS
**AI Governance | Interdisciplinary Research | Public Engagement**

Founder of AETHOS, an interdisciplinary initiative connecting students, faculty, and industry professionals around human-centered AI research, critical technological literacy, and responsible AI development.

The initiative develops opportunities for collaborative research, education, and public engagement with the societal implications of artificial intelligence.
