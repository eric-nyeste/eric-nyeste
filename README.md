#A few things about Eric and my CV 
Normative and AI Ethics | Human–AI Interaction | AI Evaluation Research

I'm a graduate researcher in Philosophy at San Francisco State University, investigating human agency, ethics and power, and the evaluation of AI-mediated discourse.

My work explores how philosophical questions about epistemic agency, technological influence, and human judgment can be translated into empirical research methods.

Current research

AI Behavioral Relativity: Evaluating conversational dominance in human–LLM interactions.

Machine learning classification and model evaluation.

Human-centered AI governance and interdisciplinary research through AETHOS.

Methods and tools: Python, scikit-learn, qualitative discourse analysis, research design, evaluation rubrics, and philosophical analysis.
